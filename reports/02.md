- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: The process of visually and statistically understanding a dataset before modeling.

- **Datetime Parsing**: Converting raw timestamps into proper time-series format.

- **Data Integrity**: Ensuring no missing values, duplicates, or corrupted observations exist.

- **Market Regime Observation**: Visual identification of trend and ranging behavior.
