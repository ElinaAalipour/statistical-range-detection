## 📚 What I Learned

- Market ranging behavior is subjective and difficult to define visually.
- Quant trading requires transforming intuition into measurable variables.
- Combining multiple weak signals may be stronger than relying on one indicator.

---

## 💡 Key Concepts

- **Market Regime**: The behavioral state of the market (trend, range, expansion, compression).

- **Feature Engineering**: Selecting measurable variables that represent market behavior.

- **Volatility Compression**: A reduction in market movement, often associated with ranging periods.

- **Range Score**: A continuous score between 0–100 representing probability of range conditions.

---

## 🐛 Problems & Solutions

- **Problem:** “Range” had no precise measurable definition.

- **Solution:** Broke the problem into measurable components:
  - volatility compression
  - price compression
  - bounded movement
